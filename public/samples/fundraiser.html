Help Maya get an accessible van

$6,000 raised of a $6,000 goal from 48 donors.

Maya is 29, lives on her own and uses a power wheelchair. She is starting community college this spring, and the bus route does not reach campus.

Here is what the money is for:

  • $5,200 for a wheelchair ramp and lock-down conversion of a used minivan, done by Lakeside Mobility Conversions.
  • $500 for the first months of insurance on the van.
  • $300 for gas for her first semester of classes.

Maya receives SSI, so we want to make sure the money reaches her in a way that does not affect her benefits.

Organized by Rosa, her mom.
